When something counts as watchable

A programme that goes out at eleven at night is not really available until the next day. This setting decides how long Nextup waits before it puts something in Next up, moves a movie into Ready to watch, and puts it in the morning email.

0 means the day it airs. 1 means the following morning, which is the usual choice if you watch things back rather than live.
