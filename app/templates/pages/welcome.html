One step to start

Nextup gets every show, movie and cover from TheTVDB or TMDB. Both give out a key for free for small projects and personal use. One key is enough, and you can add the other later.

TheTVDB

  1. Open thetvdb.com/api-information/signup and ask for a key. Choose the plan for projects earning under 50,000 dollars a year.
  2. Paste it here. It is checked before it is saved, then encrypted.

TMDB

  1. Make a free account at themoviedb.org/signup.
  2. Open themoviedb.org/settings/api, ask for a personal key, and copy the API Key (v3 auth).
  3. Paste it here. It is checked before it is saved, then encrypted.
