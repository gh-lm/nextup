Privacy

Short version: this website collects nothing about you, and the software keeps everything on your own machine.

This website

These pages are plain files. They set no cookies, run no analytics, load nothing from another company and have no forms, so there is nothing for us to collect and nothing for us to keep. We hold no personal data about anyone who reads this site. The apps menu in the top corner loads a script and its icons from apps.lightmorphic.com, which is also ours; it sets no cookies, stores nothing and sends nothing back.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, a separate company with its own privacy policy. Cloudflare receives your IP address when your browser asks for a page, in the same way any web server does, and uses it to serve the page and to guard against abuse.

The Nextup software

Nextup is something you install on your own server. We never see it, and it never contacts us. There is no account with Lightmorphic, no licence check and no usage reporting of any kind.

What your own copy stores, on your own disk:

  • The shows and movies you added, and which episodes you marked as watched.
  • Your username and a hashed password. The password itself is never stored.
  • Your TMDB API key, encrypted with a key held in the same directory.
  • If you set up the morning email: your mail server's details, its username, the sending and receiving addresses, and the mail password, which is encrypted the same way as the TMDB key.
  • Your settings, such as the country whose release dates and services are shown.
  • Artwork downloaded from TMDB, cached so pages do not call out to anyone.

All of it sits in the one directory you chose when you installed it. You are the only person who can reach it, and deleting that directory deletes everything.

What Nextup sends out

Two things, both made by your server. First, requests to TMDB asking about the shows and movies you looked up and fetching their artwork. TMDB therefore sees your server's IP address and what you searched for. Their handling of that is covered by the TMDB privacy policy. If you would rather not have that happen, do not add a TMDB key, though then the app has nothing to look up.

Second, if you turn on the morning email, the message goes to the mail server you entered, which delivers it to the address you gave. Nothing is sent to Lightmorphic or anyone else.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, write to privacy@lightmorphic.com and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
