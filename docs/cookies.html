Cookies

This website sets no cookies and does no tracking.

On this website

None. Not one, first-party or otherwise. There is no consent banner here because there is nothing to consent to. The pages are static files with a stylesheet, a font and some images, all served from this domain.

There is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no embedded video and no social widget. Nothing on these pages reports back to us or to anyone else.

In the Nextup software

Your own installation sets two cookies, both served by your own server and neither of which leaves it.

  • A session cookie. It keeps you signed in. It holds a signed session identifier, nothing more, and it is marked HttpOnly and SameSite Lax. It is deleted when you sign out.
  • A theme cookie. It remembers whether you picked light or dark, so the choice survives a reload. It holds one word and lasts a year. If you leave the app following your system setting, it is never set.

Neither is used for tracking, profiling or measurement, and neither is readable by anyone but your own server.
