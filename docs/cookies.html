Cookies

This website sets no cookies and does no tracking.

On this website

None. Not one, first-party or otherwise. There is no consent banner here because there is nothing to consent to. The pages are static files with a stylesheet, a font and some images, all served from this domain, plus the Lightmorphic apps menu in the corner, which is a script and some icons from apps.lightmorphic.com. That is ours, it sets no cookies and it stores nothing.

There is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no embedded video and no social widget. Nothing on these pages reports back to us or to anyone else.

In the Nextup software

Your own installation sets one cookie, served by your own server, which never leaves it.

  • A session cookie. It keeps you signed in. It holds a signed session identifier, nothing more, and it is marked HttpOnly and SameSite Lax. It is deleted when you sign out.

It is not used for tracking, profiling or measurement, and it is not readable by anyone but your own server.
